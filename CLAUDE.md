# CLAUDE.md — Nomad, Phase 1

**Nomad is a national veterinary ER wait-time map with a client-advocacy layer.** It helps a pet owner in an emergency find where their pet will be seen fastest — including driving further to a faster ER — and arms them to advocate for themselves once they're there. It is strictly neutral and community-first.

This file is the architecture document and standing instructions for building Phase 1. Read it fully at the start of every session. Once the codebase is scaffolded, you may propose splitting deep sections into `/docs` with pointers kept here — propose it, don't do it unilaterally.

---

## 1. Product invariants (non-negotiable)

These override any convenient implementation choice. If a task conflicts with one, stop and flag it instead of building.

1. **Neutral, always.** No ownership labeling, no corporate-vs-independent framing, no editorial judgment of any facility, anywhere in UI, data model, or copy. Nomad serves the community by being useful to everyone, not by taking sides. (This is a deliberate contrast with the founder's other product and must never bleed over.)
2. **Value at zero participation.** The map must be fully useful to the very first user with zero clinics claimed or reporting. Every facility gets a modeled estimate from day one. No blank or "unknown" pins.
3. **No verification gate.** Every facility appears on the map as soon as it's seeded and classified. Verification/claiming is optional later enrichment, never a prerequisite to be shown.
4. **Never a fake-precise live number.** Every estimate is framed as *typical/modeled* with honest uncertainty and "call to confirm" language. A wrong precise number tied to a named hospital can send someone driving the wrong way in an emergency. Modeled estimates with honest uncertainty ARE the product, not a limitation of it.
5. **Clinic self-report is the weakest signal.** Clinics are incentivized to under-report waits to hoard patients. Their reports are displayed as labeled, timestamped *claims*, hard-decayed, and cross-checked against the model — never parroted at face value.
6. **Owner reports move display toward the model, never beyond it.** Waiting-room reports are a contradiction/audit signal against rosy claims. They can revert a claim to the neutral model baseline; they can never make a facility look faster *or slower than the model says*. This bounds every gaming strategy (clinics hoarding, competitors sabotaging) at the neutral baseline.
7. **Estimates are for stable patients — critical cases go to the NEAREST open ER.** Every ER triages life-threatening cases first, so ranking a critical patient toward a farther "faster" hospital is the one way this map could hurt someone. The critical-signs banner (§10) is a permanent fixture of the ranking UI, not dismissible fine print.
8. **Phase 2 (consent-based arrival tracking) is NOT built now.** Leave the seams (§13). No location tracking, no Twilio, no push notifications, no billing logic.
9. **Seed from every available source, ownership-blind.** Mine local data first (§7.1), then scrape the open web to complete national coverage — including corporate and chain locations (VEG, BluePearl, MedVet, VCA, Thrive, etc.). Merge and dedupe. Capture name, address, coordinates, phone, type, capabilities, hours. **Never carry ownership/corporate labels into Nomad** — they are used for nothing, stored in no column, and shown nowhere (invariant #1).

**Row Level Security:**
- `facilities`, `facility_capabilities`, `facility_species`, model tables: `select` for `anon`. Writes via service role / admin only.
- `status_reports`: `insert` only where a `clinic_members` row links `auth.uid()` to that `facility_id`; `select` public.
- `owner_reports`, `facility_flags`: no direct client access. Inserts go through API routes using the service role (rate limits live there, §6.5); reads only via aggregate views that never expose `device_hash`.
- `claim_requests`: users see their own; admin sees all.
- `clinic_members`: users see their own memberships.

**Implementation note (not in the original spec):** admin identity is an `admins` table plus an `is_admin()` security-definer function. Chosen because §9 requires role-gated admin pages and nothing else defined "admin". Swap freely if you prefer a JWT claim.

## 6. Predictive wait model (the heart of Phase 1 — get this honest first)

Every facility always has a modeled estimate: *"no live data; typical wait for this facility type at this hour."* The model must feel correct to someone who works ERs — a domain-plausible estimate per facility per hour, never a flat constant.

**Single source of truth:** `lib/model/estimate.ts`, pure and unit-tested, with every parameter loaded from the DB tables above (cache params in-process ~5 min). The inspection page, the map, and the ranking all call this one function. No second implementation anywhere.

### 6.1 Formula

```
p50(facility, t) =
  clamp(
      base_minutes[type]
    × hod[type][local_hour(t, facility.tz)]
    × day_mult[day_class(local_date, holidays)]
    × shift_mult(type, local_hour)          -- 1.0 outside windows
    × facility.density_mult,
    min_minutes[type], max_minutes[type])

band = [p50 × band_lo, p50 × band_hi]       -- wider when hours_confidence = 'unknown'
```

**Local time or nothing.** `local_hour` is computed in the facility's IANA `tz`. A Maryland ER and a Texas ER at the same UTC instant are in different hours of their day. This is the classic bug — there's a required test for it (§15).

`day_class`: holiday > holiday_adjacent (day before/after a major holiday) > saturday/sunday/friday > weekday. Sunday 2 a.m. is class `sunday` (local date).

### 6.2 Seed values (v0 — tunable, not sacred)

These are starting points chosen to be directionally right; Rod calibrates them on the M1 inspection page. Structure is the contract, numbers are knobs. **Seeded by `supabase/migrations/*_seed_model_defaults.sql`.**

`base_waits`:

| type | base | min | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| er | 85 | 20 | 360 |
| er_specialty | 95 | 20 | 360 |
| urgent_care | 40 | 10 | 180 |
| specialty | — appointment-based; excluded from wait modeling and emergency ranking (§8). No `base_waits` row; `estimate.ts` returns null. |

`hod_curves` for `er` / `er_specialty` (urgent_care gets its own flatter curve over open hours, with an at-open bump and a 5–8 p.m. after-work surge):

| local hours | mult | why |
|---|---|---|
| 00–02 | 1.35 | skeleton overnight staffing, low throughput |
| 02–05 | 1.25 | same, volume tapering |
| 05–07 | 1.10 | |
| 07–09 | 1.05 | morning arrivals build |
| 09–12 | 0.60 | fully staffed, pre-surge — the fast window |
| 12–15 | 0.85 | |
| 15–17 | 1.05 | |
| 17–20 | 1.45 | after-GP-hours surge begins |
| 20–23 | 1.55 | peak evening slam |
| 23–00 | 1.45 | |

`day_mults`: weekday 1.0 · friday 1.15 · saturday 1.35 · sunday 1.50 · holiday 1.70 · holiday_adjacent 1.25.

`shift_windows` (er, er_specialty): 07–09 ×1.15 and 19–21 ×1.15 — intake stalls during 12-hour handoffs.

`holidays` seed: US federal holidays plus the vet-ER-specific calendar — **July 3–5 (fireworks: the single biggest surge of the year)**, Thanksgiving + the day after, Dec 24–26, Dec 31–Jan 1. Major class for July 4, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day; the flanking days are `holiday_adjacent`. Generated per-year by `scripts/seed/holidays.ts` so future years need no code change.

`density_mult` (precomputed per facility by a seed job; rerun after national seed): count other `active` er/er_specialty facilities within 40 km →
0 others: 1.30 · 1: 1.15 · 2–3: 1.00 · 4–6: 0.92 · 7+: 0.85. Fewer nearby options = longer waits.

Band ratios (`model_params`): `band_lo` 0.65 / `band_hi` 1.45; widen to 0.55 / 1.60 when `hours_confidence = 'unknown'`.

**Sanity anchors** (also encoded as tests): a suburban ER with 2 nearby alternatives —
- Sunday 2:30 a.m.: 85 × 1.25 × 1.50 ≈ 159 → "typically 1¾–4 hr".
- Tuesday 10 a.m.: 85 × 0.60 ≈ 51 → "typically 35 min–1¼ hr".
- July 4th, 8 p.m.: 85 × 1.55 × 1.70 × 1.15 ≈ 258 → clamps/displays as "typically 3–6 hr".

If a computed pattern would make an ER tech laugh, the model is wrong — fix the params, not the copy.

### 6.3 Display rules

- Never a bare number. Always a rounded, friendly range + provenance + freshness: *"Typically 2–3.5 hr at this hour (modeled — call to confirm)."*
- Rounding: <90 min → 15-min steps; ≥90 min → half-hour steps; ≥4 hr → "4–6 hr+".
- Closed-now facilities (per `hours`) show "Opens 8 a.m." and are excluded from "seen fastest" ranking; unknown hours on a non-24/7 type show "Hours unknown — call first."

### 6.4 Signal tiers & fusion (integrity core)

`lib/model/fuse.ts`. Each source implements one interface so future paid tiers drop in without rework:

```ts
interface WaitSignalSource {
  tier: SignalTier;                    // 'model' | 'clinic_claim' | 'owner_report' | (reserved)
  read(facilityId: string, at: Date): Promise<WaitSignal | null>;
}
interface WaitSignal {
  p50Minutes: number;  bandLo: number;  bandHi: number;
  asOf: Date;  confidence: number;      // 0..1 after decay
  meta?: { bucket?: WaitBucket; checkResult?: ClaimCheck };
}
```

**Tier 0 — model.** Always available. The floor and the neutral point everything reverts to.

**Tier 1 — clinic claim (weakest signal).**
- Latest non-superseded, non-expired `status_reports` row.
- **Decay:** full weight ≤30 min old, then linear to zero at `effective_ttl = 90 min × facility.credibility` (so a repeat under-reporter's claims die in ~45 min, a clean clinic's last 90). Expired → display reverts to model automatically. TTL knobs in `model_params`.
- **Cross-check on write and on read:** claim bucket midpoint < 0.4 × model p50 AND model p50 ≥ 60 → `check_result = 'optimistic_flag'`. Display shows *both*: "Clinic reported: under 30 min (18 min ago) · Model expects 2–3.5 hr at this hour — call to confirm." Never silently parrot the claim.
- A plausible fresh claim blends with the model proportional to its decayed confidence; it narrows the band, it doesn't replace the provenance line.
- `capacity_state` and `call_first` display as labeled, timestamped chips with the same TTL. `on_divert` / `closed_temporarily` demote the facility in ranking and get a prominent badge.

**Tier 2 — owner reports (contradiction/audit only).**
- Anonymous. Kinds: "still waiting after X", "my total wait was X", "left for a faster ER".
- **Contradiction rule:** ≥2 `active` reports from distinct `device_hash` within 3 h whose bucket ≥ claim bucket + 2 steps, **or** 1 such report against a claim already carrying `optimistic_flag` → claim becomes `contradicted`: display reverts to the model + "Recent visitors report longer waits than the clinic's update."
- **Credibility:** each contradicted claim: `credibility − 0.10` (floor 0.25); recovers +0.02 per week without a contradicted claim (cap 1.0). Lower credibility → shorter claim TTLs and easier flags. **The map gets more honest about a facility the more it under-reports.**
- **Bounded influence (invariant #6):** owner reports only ever move the display *back toward the model*. They never produce an estimate above or below it. Spam against a rival clinic accomplishes nothing worse than the neutral baseline.
- Clinic dashboards show their own contradiction flags privately — transparency nudges honesty.

### 6.5 Anti-abuse (verification-lite)

- `device_hash` = salted hash of a stable client token + coarse IP; salt in env; never displayed or exported.
- Rate limits enforced in the API route (service role): 1 owner report per device per facility per 4 h; 5 per device per day; 3 flags per device per day. Enforce by counting recent rows — no extra infra.
- Reports auto-expire from fusion after 4 h (they're waiting-room observations, not facts about tomorrow).
- Optional SMS-verified reports (heavier weight) are a listed future add — do not build in Phase 1.

## 7. Facility seeding pipeline (`scripts/seed/`)

One national registry of every US veterinary ER, ER+specialty, specialty, and urgent-care facility. No manual gate to appear (invariant #3). Precision over recall for the *emergency* layers: a facility only gets `facility_type` of `er`/`er_specialty`/`urgent_care` on strong signals (licensing category, 24/7 hours, explicit emergency naming/services). Ambiguous candidates go in as `needs_review` and are excluded from emergency ranking until classified — showing a daytime GP as a 2 a.m. ER is the failure mode to avoid, and it is classification, not gatekeeping.

### 7.1 Seed source reality check (measured 2026-08-10 — read before planning seed work)

**`~/Documents/review-zip-tier-mismatch.csv` — 71 rows, ~0 usable.** It is a Bravo *PE-ownership review artifact*, not a facility directory: rows are GP practices flagged for a ZIP/name collision against a corporate-owned practice. ### 7.2 Sources

Ordered by durability. **Rod has explicitly authorized broad web scraping (2026-08-10) — it is a required capability, not a fallback.** The measurements above are why: without it there is no DMV seed and no ER coverage.

1. **State veterinary board facility/premise license rosters** — public records; the clean backbone. Per-state importer modules in `scripts/seed/states/`; coverage varies by state (not all license premises) and that's fine. M1 ships MD, DC, VA.
2. **OpenStreetMap** (Overpass: `amenity=veterinary` + emergency-signal tags/names) — ODbL: attribute OSM in the app footer, keep OSM IDs in `seed_sources` so OSM-derived rows stay isolatable if licensing review ever requires it.
3. **Web scrape — chain/corporate location directories and individual facility sites.** Every large ER group publishes its own location list; these are the highest-yield source for exactly the facilities boards and Bravo miss. Scrape name, address, phone, hours, capabilities, coordinates. Record the source URL and retrieval time in `seed_sources`. **Ownership is not captured** — a VEG location and an independent ER are indistinguishable rows in Nomad (invariant #1).
   - Operational hygiene, not gatekeeping: identify the crawler honestly, rate-limit politely, cache raw responses under `scripts/seed/.cache/` so re-runs don't re-fetch, and keep each site's parser isolated so one layout change breaks one importer.
4. **Google Places API — transient enrichment only.** Places terms prohibit persisting most content beyond a short refreshable cache; only the `place_id` may be stored long-term. So: store `google_place_id`, use Places at runtime/refresh to fill gaps (hours, phone) without persisting those fields as authoritative. **Do not build a Places→registry bulk copier** — this one is a technical constraint on where durable data comes from, unrelated to the scraping decision above.
5. **Clinic self-submission** ("Add your facility") — lands as `needs_review` for classification, then `active`.

**Pipeline stages (idempotent, provenance-preserving):** import → normalize (name casing, address, phone E.164) → geocode where the source lacks coordinates → derive `tz` from location → classify `facility_type` → dedupe → write with `seed_sources` appended, never overwritten.

**Dedupe:** candidate pairs = trigram name similarity ≥ 0.45 AND within 300 m. Auto-merge exact matches; otherwise queue in `/admin/review` (mark the loser `duplicate`, merge `seed_sources`). Never hard-delete.

**Post-seed jobs:** `density_mult` precompute; holidays seed; a monthly re-run of importers diffing against existing rows (report adds/changes to admin, don't blind-overwrite).

### 7.3 Autonomy target

The end state is a self-supporting site: seeding and refresh run on a schedule with no human in the loop for the common case. Every importer is therefore written to be **idempotent, resumable, and diff-reporting** from day one — a re-run must be safe, cheap, and produce a change report rather than a silent overwrite. Scheduling itself (cron/queue) lands in M2; do not build it earlier, but do not write importers that would need rewriting to be scheduled.

## 8. Drive time + fastest-care ranking

**`DriveTimeProvider`** (`lib/drivetime/`):

```ts
interface DriveTimeProvider {
  minutes(origin: LatLng, dests: LatLng[]): Promise<number[]>;
}
```

- `heuristicProvider` (default, zero cost): haversine × 1.30 road-winding factor ÷ speed — 45 km/h where `density_mult ≤ 0.95` (metro), 65 km/h otherwise. Label output "~drive".
- `osrmProvider` / `mapboxMatrixProvider`: selected by `DRIVE_TIME_PROVIDER` env var when a key/endpoint is configured. Same interface, no other code changes.

**Ranking (`GET /api/rank`):**
1. Candidates: `active` facilities within 120 km (PostGIS `ST_DWithin`, cap 25), filtered by requested species/capabilities, currently open (or 24/7), `facility_type in (er, er_specialty, urgent_care)`. `specialty` never ranks — it renders as "Referral/specialty — not a walk-in ER."
2. Score = drive minutes + fused wait p50. `on_divert`/`at_capacity`/`closed_temporarily` sink to the bottom with their badge (still visible — an owner may have no other option).
3. Return per-facility breakdown: drive, wait band, total-until-seen band, provenance, freshness, badges.
4. **Further-but-faster callout:** when a non-nearest facility beats the nearest by ≥30 min total, surface it explicitly: *"14 min further to drive — likely seen ~1 hr sooner."* This sentence is the product's thesis; give it visual weight.

## 10. Map, ranking & advocacy UI

Assume the primary user is standing in a parking lot at 2 a.m. holding a sick animal and a phone. Mobile-first, one-handed, glanceable, fast. Dark mode is not a nice-to-have — it's the 2 a.m. default.

### 10.1 Map
- MapLibre, clustered pins. Pin encodes the current estimate band by **color + shape/label redundantly** (colorblind-safe; never color alone). Divert/closed states get distinct badges.
- Tap → bottom-sheet card: name, type, capacity/divert chip, estimate band with provenance + "updated X ago", `call_first` chip, capability chips, **Call** (`tel:`) and **Directions** (native maps deeplink) as the two dominant actions.
- Filters (persist in URL): species, capabilities (overnight, exotics, oxygen, isolation, ER surgery, endoscopy…), open now, hide on-divert.
- Every status field shows its freshness. Decayed/expired data visibly reverts to the modeled estimate — staleness is displayed, never silently served.

### 10.2 Facility detail (`/er/[state]/[city]/[slug]`)
Everything on the card plus hours (+ confidence), full provenance line ("Modeled estimate" / "Clinic-reported, cross-checked"), the advocacy module (§10.5), a "Something wrong? Flag it" link, and a low-key "Work here? Claim this listing" CTA.

### 10.3 Fastest-care ranking ("Find fastest care now")
- One giant primary action on the home screen → location permission → ranked list per §8.
- Each row: **total time until seen** (drive + wait, as a band) as the headline number, with the breakdown beneath. Further-but-faster rows get the callout chip.
- The ranking header carries the standing line: *"Estimates are typical for stable pets — always call ahead while driving."*

### 10.4 Critical-signs banner (safety invariant #7 — permanent, not dismissible)
Pinned above the ranking: **"Is your pet critical? Go to the NEAREST open ER now and call while driving — every ER sees life-threatening cases first."** Expandable list of red flags (draft for Rod's review before launch): trouble breathing or blue/pale gums · collapse or unresponsiveness · uncontrolled bleeding · seizures lasting minutes or repeating · unproductive retching with a swollen belly (possible bloat/GDV) · a male cat straining and unable to urinate · known toxin ingestion · heatstroke · major trauma (hit by car, falls) · birthing trouble. Tapping "My pet is critical" bypasses ranking → nearest open ER + Call button. Recognition and routing only — no treatment advice, ever.

### 10.5 Advocacy layer (equal in importance to the map)
Owners hesitate to push back in an ER lobby. Nomad's job is to hand them the questions and the permission. Content lives as MDX in `content/advocacy/` (easy for Rod to edit; flag for his review), rendered as a module on facility detail + ranking, plus a standalone `/advocate` page.

- **Call script (copyable):** "How many cases are ahead of me?" · "What's a realistic wait for a stable pet right now?" · "Are you on divert or at capacity?" · "Would another ER nearby see us sooner?"
- **Your options, plainly:** You can ask where you are in line and ask again if things change. If the wait is long and your pet is stable, you can transfer — ask for records and X-rays to go, and call the next ER while driving; a less-busy hospital will usually gladly take the transfer. Tell the front desk immediately if symptoms worsen — asking to be re-triaged is normal.
- **Voice:** calm, warm, shoulder-to-shoulder. Community-over-shareholders framing at the *system* level only — never a judgment about any specific facility (invariant #1).

### 10.6 Copy standards
Sentence case, plain verbs, no filler. Statuses always carry timestamps. "Call to confirm" is a standing UI pattern, not a footnote disclaimer. Buttons say what they do ("Call this ER", "Get directions"). Errors say what happened and what to do next. Nothing alarmist: red is reserved exclusively for the critical-signs banner.

## 12. Design direction

Warm, trustworthy, community-serving — a neighbor who happens to know every ER in the country, not a corporate healthtech dashboard and not an alarm console.

- **Ground it in the subject:** late-night emergencies, waiting rooms, community care. Legibility under stress is the aesthetic: generous type scale, huge tap targets, high contrast, calm surfaces, disciplined color.
- **Signature element suggestion — make honesty visible:** the provenance/freshness treatment as a design language. Estimates visibly carry their source and age; claims visually decay and hand back to the model. If Nomad is remembered for one visual idea, it should be that the map is honest about what it knows.
- **Avoid the AI-default looks** (cream background + high-contrast serif + terracotta accent; near-black + single acid-green accent; broadsheet hairlines everywhere). Pick a palette and type pairing specific to this brief; take one deliberate aesthetic risk and keep everything else quiet.
- **Use the refero MCP** before building UI: pull 3–5 real references — emergency/urgent-care finders, honest-status products (outage pages, trail-conditions apps, transit arrival boards), community mutual-aid tools. Commit to a direction in writing (palette hexes, type roles, layout concept, the signature) before coding it.
- Quality floor without announcing it: responsive to small phones, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion respected, WCAG-AA contrast, dark mode first-class.
- **The M0 shell is deliberately undesigned.** Do not treat its default Tailwind styling as a direction; the design pass happens with references, per the above.

## 15. Testing expectations

`npx tsc --noEmit` clean before every push (standing rule). Vitest for units. Priorities:

**Model invariants (`estimate.ts`):**
- ER overnight (2 a.m.) > ER mid-morning (10 a.m.), same facility/day-class.
- Sunday > Tuesday; July 4 > ordinary Sunday; holiday_adjacent between weekday and holiday.
- Density monotonic: fewer nearby ERs → longer estimate, all else equal.
- Clamps hold under multiplier stacking; band_lo < p50 < band_hi always.
- **Timezone:** a Maryland and a Texas facility evaluated at the same UTC instant use different local hours; DST transition dates don't crash or double-count.
- The §6.2 sanity anchors as snapshot tests (update deliberately when Rod re-tunes).

**Fusion (`fuse.ts`):**
- Claim confidence decays to 0 at effective TTL; expired claims revert display to model.
- Optimistic flag fires exactly on threshold; plausible claims blend, flagged claims show both.
- Contradiction requires 2 distinct devices (or 1 + flag); contradicted claims revert to model — and owner reports can never push an estimate beyond the model in either direction.
- Credibility floors at 0.25, recovers on schedule, and shortens TTL as specified.

**Seeding:** importers idempotent (re-run = no dupes); dedupe candidates queue instead of auto-merging when uncertain; `seed_sources` append-only.

**Rate limits:** the §6.5 windows enforced and off-by-one tested.
